Question 1: What do you envision yourself doing 5 minutes before class starts each day, to ensure that you are ready to start on time?

Everyone is getting their tech ready or already has it prepared by the time class starts. Some are reviewing notes or getting their drinks ready to stay alert. The default summary is not helpful. Since this answer is open-ended, the summary missed the context of what the question was asking. It states that everyone answered what they do minutes before class, but that is not a clear answer. One challenge that comes with written responses is having to read through each answer to understand the context. Some responses contain grammatical or syntax errors, which can skew the results. Some people did provide similar responses, which was reflected in the summary. The question could have been formatted as a poll with guided responses to gain a clearer understanding of what people are doing without having to read every response for clarification. Another option could have been added for answers that do not align with the predetermined responses.

9. Again, pretend you are the instructor. Do you think it was helpful or not helpful to make this survey anonymous? Why or why not?

I think it was helpful to make the survey anonymous. There are many advantages to anonymous surveys. People may be more honest if they feel that their responses cannot be traced back to them. They can provide feedback that they might not otherwise share if their names were attached to their responses. The only disadvantage I can think of is that individuals who may need personal assistance could go unnoticed because it would be difficult to identify who they are.
